# Validation roadmap dataset descriptions

The [last slide of the ShapeMix High School Research Deck](../presentations/ShapeMix_High_School_Research_Deck.pptx) is a validation roadmap. Although it has four rows, the final row combines two accessions, so it represents five dataset families.

“Size” below means cells for single-cell data and spatial pixels for tissue data.

| Dataset | Species and biological material | Data type | Size used by ShapeMix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main PBMC** | Human peripheral blood mononuclear cells from one healthy 25-year-old female donor; granulocytes were removed | 10x Multiome: paired single-nucleus ATAC-seq and RNA-seq | Original sample: **11,898 nuclei**. Benchmark subset: **9,500 labeled cells across 16 immune cell types**. Five 70/30 splits give 6,644 reference and 2,856 held-out cells. There are 20 benchmark datasets, each with 1,024 simulated spots and about 10 cells per spot. [10x source](https://cf.10xgenomics.com/samples/cell-arc/2.0.0/pbmc_granulocyte_sorted_10k/pbmc_granulocyte_sorted_10k_web_summary.html); [local protocol](../benchmark_protocol.md#2-dataset-and-admissible-cells) |
| **GSE129785** | The selected subset is **human blood/PBMC and sorted immune cells**. The full GEO accession also contains tumors, bone marrow, and human/mouse cell-line experiments, but those are excluded here | Single-cell ATAC-seq. Includes sorted dendritic cells, monocytes, B cells, Tregs, naive/memory CD4 T cells, NK cells, and naive/memory CD8 T cells | **30 selected samples**: 14 physical dilution mixtures, 9 sorted reference populations, 4 PBMC replicates, and 3 fresh/frozen preparation samples. The project retains **497,297 barcode observations**; the high-confidence sorted reference contains **14,688 author-called cells**. Downloaded payload: 37.3 GiB. [GEO study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE129785); [local record](gse129785_preprocessing.md) |
| **GSE194122** | Human **bone-marrow mononuclear cells**, or BMMCs, from healthy donors | Two single-cell multi-omic arms exist: ATAC+RNA Multiome and RNA+surface-protein CITE-seq. ShapeMix uses the ATAC+RNA Multiome arm | Whole study: **12 donors and four processing sites**. ShapeMix Multiome arm: **69,249 cells**, **10 donors**, and 13 donor/site batches, with 22 cell-type annotations, 116,490 ATAC peaks, and 13,431 genes. [NCBI BioProject](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA799242); [local record](gse194122_preprocessing.md) |
| **GSE205055** | Both mouse and human intact tissue: **E13 mouse whole embryo**, **P21/P22 mouse brain**, and **adult human hippocampus** | Spatial ATAC-RNA, with matched histone/CUT&Tag information for several groups. Spatial resolution is 20, 25, or 50 µm | **Six ATAC-bearing spatial groups**, totaling **29,966 ATAC-positive pixels** and 481.8 million ATAC fragment rows. The complete family has 22 samples; raw archive size is 8.18 GB. [Primary publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10076218/); [local record](gse205055_gse263333_preprocessing.md) |
| **GSE263333** | Mouse tissue. The ShapeMix-relevant ATAC sections are **E13 whole embryo** and **five-month-old EAE brain**; EAE is a mouse model of inflammatory neurological disease | Spatial-Mux-seq combining spatial ATAC with RNA, histone modifications, and—in selected sections—protein measurements | **Two ATAC-bearing sections**: 2,500 embryo pixels at 50 µm and 10,000 EAE-brain pixels at 20 µm, totaling **12,500 pixels** and 133.7 million ATAC fragment rows. Complete series: 12 samples; raw archive: 3.31 GB. [GEO study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263333); [local record](gse205055_gse263333_preprocessing.md) |

The key experimental distinction is:

- **Main PBMC:** exact mixture proportions are known because each pseudo-spot is constructed from recorded cells.
- **GSE129785:** dilution ratios are known only nominally; sorting and recovery can alter the realized proportions.
- **GSE194122:** exact truth can be created by constructing pseudo-spots from a held-out donor.
- **GSE205055 and GSE263333:** these are real tissue sections, so exact cell-type proportions are unavailable. Validation instead uses anatomy, RNA, proteins, histone marks, tissue images, and marker accessibility. They are currently source-ready but still require compatible labeled single-cell ATAC references before ShapeMix analysis.
