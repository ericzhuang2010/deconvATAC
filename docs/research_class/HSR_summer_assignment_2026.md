### 2.2 Simplified Proposal Example

#### Broad Goal

Dysfunction of gap junctions plays a role in diseases of the heart and liver; treatments that could reduce this dysfunction could be important for a variety of conditions.

#### Specific Goal

Gap junction communication also appears to play a role in planaria regeneration. The goal of this study is to use planaria as a model system to test the ability of GABA treatment to restore regenerative polarity by overcoming gap junction inhibition.

#### Hypothesis

We hypothesized that post-pharyngeal planaria fragments treated with 127 µM octanol would exhibit a bipolar (two-headed) phenotype, but fragments treated with 127 µM octanol in addition to varying concentrations of GABA would regenerate with a normal phenotype. Under these conditions, GABA would be able to excite action potentials in immature neurons and encourage chemical synapses to restore proper anteroposterior axial polarity and offer potential curative treatments for diseases with disrupted communication.

#### Methods

| Variable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Independent variables** | GABA treatment and concentration of treatment |
| **Dependent variable** | Rating of planaria regeneration as either a normal or two-headed phenotype |

> **Required:** Include a summary of the methods you will use to conduct the study and measure your dependent variables.
